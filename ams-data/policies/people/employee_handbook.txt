1. ABOUT THIS HANDBOOK
1.1 Purpose
This handbook brings together everything AMS employees need to know about working at AMS: the company, joining and leaving, leave and holidays, hybrid work, benefits, pay, performance, tax, and raising concerns. Policies for other departments are in their own handbooks: IT and Security, Finance, Legal and Compliance, Administration and Facilities, and Learning and Development.

1.2 Who this handbook applies to
It applies to all regular employees in the Bengaluru, Pune, and Austin offices. Where India and US rules differ, the chapter says which applies. Interns and contractors follow their contract and only the chapters that name them.

1.4 Definitions
Base office: the office named in the employee's contract. It decides the payroll, leave policy, and holiday calendar that apply.
India payroll and US payroll: employees in Bengaluru and Pune are on the India payroll; employees in Austin are on the US payroll.
Grade: one of the six job grades, L1 to L6, described in chapter 2.
Manager: the employee's reporting manager in AMS People.
Department head: the director (grade L6) responsible for the department.
Financial year: 1 April to 31 March. Leave follows the calendar year unless a chapter says otherwise.
AMS People: the HR portal, used for leave, payslips, personal details, and requests.

1.5 Roles and responsibilities
Employees read this handbook, follow it, and keep their details in AMS People up to date.
Managers apply the handbook fairly and approve or decline requests in AMS People on time.
People Operations owns this handbook, answers questions within 2 working days, and updates it when policies change.
Payroll pays salaries and handles tax deductions in both countries.
Department heads approve exceptions where a chapter allows them.

2. COMPANY OVERVIEW AND EMPLOYEE GUIDE
Reference: HR-OVR-2026 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 1 April 2026
2.1 ABOUT AMS
AMS is a software and IT services company that builds and supports enterprise applications for clients in banking, retail, and healthcare. AMS employs approximately 1,200 people across three offices in two countries. The company headquarters is in Bengaluru.
The company's financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Some benefits, such as the learning budget in India, follow the financial year, while leave follows the calendar year. Each policy states which year it uses.

2.2 OFFICES AND PAYROLL
2.2.1 Office locations
AMS has three offices:
Bengaluru, India | Headquarters | About 700 employees | India payroll
Pune, India | Delivery centre | About 350 employees | India payroll
Austin, Texas, United States | Client office | About 150 employees | US payroll
2.2.2 Base location
Every employee has a base location, which is the office named in their employment contract. The base location decides which payroll, leave policy, and holiday calendar apply. Working temporarily from another city or country does not change the base location. Employees on the India payroll follow the India leave policy (HR-LEAVE-IN-2026). Employees on the US payroll follow the US leave policy (HR-LEAVE-US-2026).
2.2.3 Changing base location
A permanent move between offices is a transfer, not remote work. Transfers need approval from the current manager, the receiving manager, and People Operations. A transfer between India and the United States also needs a new employment contract and immigration review.

2.3 JOB GRADES
2.3.1 Grade structure
AMS uses six job grades. Grades are used in several policies, including travel limits, notice periods, and approval rules.
L1 | Associate | Entry level, usually up to two years of experience
L2 | Engineer or Analyst | Works independently on defined tasks
L3 | Senior Engineer or Senior Analyst | Leads features and mentors juniors
L4 | Team Lead | Leads a team of up to ten people; first level of people management
L5 | Manager or Principal | Manages several teams or is a senior individual contributor
L6 | Director | Leads a department or a major client account
2.3.2 People managers
Employees at grade L4 and above may be people managers. Where a policy says "manager approval", it means the employee's reporting manager as shown in AMS People. Where a policy says "department head", it means the L6 director responsible for the department.
2.3.3 Promotions
Promotions are reviewed twice a year, in April and October, as part of the performance cycle. A promotion takes effect from the first day of the following month.

2.4 DEPARTMENTS
AMS is organised into the following departments: Engineering, Product, Delivery, Sales, Finance, People Operations, IT and Security, Legal and Compliance, and Facilities. People Operations is the HR team. Legal and Compliance owns the Code of Conduct. Finance owns the travel and expense policy.

2.5 SYSTEMS EMPLOYEES USE
2.5.1 AMS People
AMS People is the HR portal. Employees use it to apply for leave, check leave balances, view payslips, update personal and bank details, raise People Operations requests, and request remote work. Policies may refer to it as "the HR portal".
2.5.2 AMS Expense
AMS Expense is the system for travel approvals and expense claims. Claims are submitted on form EXP-F-07 inside AMS Expense.
2.5.3 AMS Learn
AMS Learn is the training platform. Mandatory trainings, such as Code of Conduct, information security, and anti-harassment training, are assigned and tracked in AMS Learn.
2.5.4 ServiceDesk
ServiceDesk is the IT support system. Employees raise tickets for laptop problems, access requests, and lost devices.

2.6 WORKING HOURS
2.6.1 Standard working week
The standard working week is Monday to Friday in all offices. A standard working day is eight hours, plus a one-hour meal break. Core collaboration hours are set in the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.
2.6.2 Overtime
Employees in India at grades L1 and L2 who are asked to work on a weekend or public holiday receive a compensatory day off, as described in the Holiday Calendar. Non-exempt employees in the United States are paid overtime as required by law.

3. ONBOARDING AND EXIT POLICY
Reference: HR-ONX-2026 | Version: 2.0 | Effective: 1 April 2026
3.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy covers what happens before and after an employee joins AMS, the probation period, resignation and notice periods, the exit process, and the final settlement. India and US rules differ in some sections; each section says which applies.

3.2 BEFORE JOINING
3.2.1 Documents
New joiners upload their identity proof, address proof, education certificates, and the relieving letter from their previous employer to AMS People at least 3 working days before the joining date.
3.2.2 Background verification
AMS carries out background verification of education, previous employment, and identity. An offer may be withdrawn if the verification finds false information.

3.3 FIRST WEEKS
3.3.1 First day
On the first day, new joiners receive their laptop and access badge and attend the company induction. Each new joiner is assigned a buddy from their team for the first 90 days.
3.3.2 Mandatory training
New joiners complete Code of Conduct, information security, and anti-harassment training in AMS Learn within 30 days of joining.

3.4 PROBATION
3.4.1 Length
Employees on the India payroll serve a probation period of 6 months. Employees on the US payroll have a 90-day introductory period.
3.4.2 Extension
Probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months, if the manager and People Operations agree that more time is needed. The employee is told in writing before the original probation ends.
3.4.3 Confirmation
Employees on the India payroll receive a confirmation letter in AMS People when probation is completed successfully.
3.4.4 Leave during probation
Annual leave builds up from the joining date. During probation, employees on the India payroll may use up to 5 days of annual leave; the rest becomes available after confirmation. Sick leave and casual leave can be used during probation.
3.4.5 Notice during probation
During probation, the notice period on either side is 15 days for employees on the India payroll.

3.5 RESIGNATION AND NOTICE PERIODS
3.5.1 How to resign
Employees resign by submitting a resignation request in AMS People. The notice period starts on the day the request is submitted.
3.5.2 Notice periods in India (after confirmation)
Grade | Notice period
L1 to L3 | 30 days
L4 and L5 | 60 days
L6 | 90 days
3.5.3 Notice in the United States
Employment in the United States is at will. AMS asks employees to give at least 2 weeks' notice, or 4 weeks' notice for grades L5 and L6.
3.5.4 Shortening the notice period
In India, the notice period may be shortened with approval from the manager and People Operations. If it is shortened at the employee's request, the employee pays basic pay for the unserved days, which is recovered in the final settlement. Unused annual leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period without this approval.
3.5.5 Leave during the notice period
Sick leave may be taken during the notice period. Annual leave during the notice period needs manager approval and is normally granted only for days already approved before the resignation.

3.6 EXIT PROCESS
3.6.1 Handover
The employee completes a handover plan agreed with their manager before the last working day.
3.6.2 Exit interview
People Operations invites every leaver to a confidential exit interview in the final two weeks.
3.6.3 Return of assets
The laptop, access badge, corporate card, and any other company property must be returned on or before the last working day. Access to AMS systems ends at 18:00 local time on the last working day.

3.7 FINAL SETTLEMENT
3.7.1 India
The full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day. It includes:
Salary up to the last working day.
Encashment of unused annual leave, up to a maximum of 15 days, at basic pay.
Gratuity, if the employee completed at least five years of continuous service.
Recovery of any unserved notice period, unreturned assets, or outstanding advances.
Casual leave, sick leave, and restricted holidays are not encashed. Leave encashment on exit is the payment on separation referred to in section 4.4.4 (Employee Leave Policy (India)).
3.7.2 United States
The final paycheck is paid on the next regular payday after the last working day, or earlier where state law requires. Unused PTO is paid out up to 10 days under section 5.4.4 (US leave policy, HR-LEAVE-US-2026). Sick leave and floating holidays are not paid out.

3.8 LETTERS AFTER LEAVING
In India, the relieving letter and experience letter are issued within 7 days of the final settlement. In the United States, an employment verification letter is provided on request.

3.9 REHIRING
Former employees may be considered for rehire 6 months after their last working day. Employees whose employment ended because of misconduct are not eligible for rehire.

3.10 CONTACT
For questions about onboarding or exit, contact People Operations through AMS People.

4. EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (INDIA)
Reference: HR-LEAVE-IN-2026 | Version: 2.1 | Effective: 1 April 2026 | Supersedes: Version 2.0
4.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy describes annual leave, sick leave, casual leave, parental leave, public holidays, carry-over, and leave requests. It applies to permanent full-time employees unless a section says otherwise. Interns, contractors, consultants, and employees paid through another country's payroll follow separate agreements and policies. Where local law or an individual employment agreement provides a more favourable entitlement, People Operations must review the case and apply the applicable requirement.

4.2 LEAVE YEAR AND GENERAL RULES
The leave year runs from 1 January through 31 December. Leave balances are recorded in days. A standard leave day represents the employee's normal scheduled working day. Employees on non-standard schedules should have their entitlement converted to the appropriate equivalent by People Operations.
Weekends and company public holidays are not deducted from annual leave when they fall within an approved leave period, unless the employee's work schedule or applicable local rule requires different treatment.
Employees should submit leave requests through the HR portal. A request is not approved merely because it has been submitted. The HR portal must show approved, or written manager confirmation must be obtained if the portal is unavailable.

4.3 ANNUAL LEAVE
4.3.1 Standard entitlement
Eligible permanent full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave for each complete leave year. Annual leave is intended for planned time away from work.
4.3.2 Joining during the year
Employees who join after 1 January receive a prorated annual-leave balance for the remainder of the leave year. The HR system calculates the balance based on the employee's start date and configured proration rules. Questions about the displayed calculation should be raised with People Operations.
4.3.3 Part-time schedules
Employees working reduced schedules receive a prorated entitlement based on their contracted working pattern. People Operations confirms the equivalent balance in the HR system.
4.3.4 Annual leave approval
Employees should submit planned annual leave at least 10 calendar days before the first day of leave. Managers should normally respond within 3 working days. These are planning targets, not automatic approval rules.
For an absence of 5 or more consecutive working days, employees should submit the request at least 15 calendar days in advance where reasonably possible. Business-critical periods may require coordination with the manager, but managers should consider requests fairly and explain any denial or proposed alternative.

4.4 ANNUAL LEAVE CARRY-OVER
4.4.1 Standard carry-over limit
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual-leave days from one leave year into the next leave year.
4.4.2 Conditions
Only unused annual leave from the current leave year is eligible for standard carry-over. Sick leave and casual leave cannot be converted into annual leave and cannot be carried over under this section.
The carried-over balance must be used by 31 March of the following year. Any standard carried-over annual leave remaining after 31 March expires, unless an approved exception under Section 4.3 applies.
4.4.3 Exception: business-critical inability to take leave
If an employee could not reasonably take annual leave because a manager required the employee to support a documented business-critical delivery, the employee may request an exception. The manager must provide written justification, and People Operations must approve the exception in writing before the normal expiry date.
An exception does not automatically grant unlimited carry-over. The approval must specify the additional number of days, the revised expiry date, and the reason for the exception. Employees should retain written approval for their records.
4.4.4 No automatic cash conversion
Unused annual leave does not automatically convert to cash at year-end under this policy. Any payment on separation or other cash settlement must be reviewed under the employee's applicable agreement and local requirements by People Operations and Payroll.

4.5 SICK LEAVE
4.5.1 Standard entitlement
Eligible permanent full-time employees receive 10 days of sick leave per leave year.
4.5.2 Carry-over
Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next leave year under this policy. Sick leave also cannot be converted into annual leave.
4.5.3 Notice and documentation
Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible when illness prevents them from working. If an absence lasts more than 3 consecutive working days, the employee may be asked to provide reasonable supporting documentation in accordance with company procedures and applicable requirements.
Medical details should be shared only through approved confidential channels and only to the extent necessary. Managers should not request detailed diagnoses in ordinary team communications.
4.5.4 Extended illness
For an illness expected to require an extended absence, the employee should contact People Operations to discuss applicable leave options and required documentation. The outcome depends on the circumstances and applicable requirements; this policy does not promise a particular accommodation or payment.

4.6 CASUAL LEAVE
4.6.1 Standard entitlement
Eligible permanent full-time employees receive 6 days of casual leave per leave year for short, unplanned personal matters.
4.6.2 Carry-over and conversion
Unused casual leave expires at the end of the leave year. It cannot be carried over and cannot be converted into annual leave or cash under this policy.
4.6.3 Request process
Employees should inform their manager as soon as practical. For an unexpected absence, advance notice is not always possible; employees should submit or regularize the request in the HR portal when able to do so.

4.7 PARENTAL LEAVE
4.7.1 General approach
Parental-leave entitlements depend on the employee's circumstances, applicable local requirements, and the company's separate parental-leave procedures. Employees should contact People Operations before planning parental leave so eligibility rules, documents, and dates can be confirmed.
4.7.2 No inference from annual leave
Annual-leave, sick-leave, and casual-leave balances do not determine parental-leave eligibility. Employees should not use the general leave balances in this policy to estimate parental-leave entitlement.

4.8 REQUESTS, APPROVALS, AND CANCELLATIONS
4.8.1 Planned requests
Submit planned leave in the HR portal and wait for the status to show approved. Managers assess timing, team coverage, and competing requests. Managers should communicate decisions and, where possible, suggest alternative dates if a request cannot be approved as submitted.
4.8.2 Urgent or emergency absence
For an emergency, contact the manager as soon as reasonably possible using the usual team communication channel. Enter the leave request in the HR portal when practical. The manager or People Operations can help regularize the record if the employee cannot access the portal.
4.8.3 Changes and cancellations
If dates change, update the HR portal and notify the manager. Employees should not assume that changing a submitted request automatically changes an existing approval.
4.8.4 Manager unavailability
If the manager is unavailable and the matter is time-sensitive, contact the manager's designated delegate or People Operations. This does not remove the need to record the request in the HR portal when access is restored.

4.9 HOLIDAYS AND WORK SCHEDULES
The company publishes a location-specific public-holiday calendar each year. Employees should consult the calendar applicable to their work location rather than relying on another office's calendar.
A public holiday is not deducted from annual leave when it falls on a scheduled working day covered by the published holiday calendar, subject to applicable local rules and the employee's work arrangement.
Employees working shifts or non-standard schedules should confirm holiday treatment with People Operations if the HR system's balance appears inconsistent with their schedule.

4.10 LEAVE BALANCE QUESTIONS AND CORRECTIONS
The HR portal is the operational source for an employee's current displayed leave balance. Employees who believe the balance is incorrect should raise a request with People Operations and include the relevant dates and leave type.
Managers should not manually promise a balance correction. People Operations reviews the request and coordinates with Payroll or the HR-system administrator when required.
For carry-over questions, include the leave year, leave type, displayed unused balance, and any written exception approval. This helps distinguish standard carry-over from an approved exception.

4.12 CONTACT
For questions about eligibility, balances, carry-over exceptions, or policy interpretation, contact People Operations through the internal HR portal. Do not send confidential medical information through public or general team channels.

5. EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (UNITED STATES)
Reference: HR-LEAVE-US-2026 | Version: 1.3 | Effective: 1 January 2026
5.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy describes paid time off (PTO), sick leave, floating holidays, parental leave, bereavement leave, jury duty, and leave requests for regular full-time employees on the US payroll. Employees on the India payroll follow the Employee Leave Policy (India), HR-LEAVE-IN-2026, even when they travel to or work from the United States. Where federal, state, or local law provides a greater benefit, the law applies.

5.2 LEAVE YEAR AND GENERAL RULES
5.2.1 Leave year
The leave year runs from 1 January through 31 December.
5.2.2 Units
Leave is recorded in days. One day equals eight working hours. Employees may take PTO in half-day units.
5.2.3 Requests
Employees request all leave in AMS People. Leave is approved only when AMS People shows the status as approved.

5.3 PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
5.3.1 Standard entitlement
Regular full-time employees receive 15 days of PTO per leave year. PTO is used for vacation, personal matters, and any other planned time away. There is no separate casual leave in the United States.
5.3.2 Service-based increase
The PTO entitlement increases with completed years of service: 18 days per leave year after 3 completed years, and 20 days per leave year after 5 completed years. The increase applies from the leave year after the anniversary.
5.3.3 Joining during the year
Employees who join after 1 January receive a prorated PTO balance based on their start date.
5.3.4 Part-time employees
Regular part-time employees receive PTO prorated to their scheduled hours.
5.3.5 PTO requests
Employees should request PTO at least 7 calendar days in advance. For an absence of 5 or more consecutive working days, employees should request PTO at least 21 calendar days in advance.

5.4 PTO CARRY-OVER AND PAYOUT
5.4.1 Carry-over limit
Employees may carry over up to 10 unused PTO days from one leave year into the next leave year.
5.4.2 Use-by date
Carried-over PTO must be used by 30 June of the following year. Carried-over PTO remaining after 30 June is forfeited.
5.4.3 No cash-out during employment
PTO cannot be exchanged for cash while employment continues.
5.4.4 Payout on separation
When employment ends, AMS pays out accrued, unused PTO up to a maximum of 10 days in the final paycheck. Sick leave and floating holidays are not paid out.

5.5 SICK LEAVE
5.5.1 Standard entitlement
Regular full-time employees receive 5 days of paid sick leave per leave year, separate from PTO. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness or medical appointments, or to care for an ill family member.
5.5.2 Carry-over
Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next leave year.
5.5.3 Documentation
A doctor's note may be requested for an absence of more than 3 consecutive working days.

5.6 FLOATING HOLIDAYS
Regular full-time employees receive 2 floating holidays per leave year, in addition to the company holidays listed in the Holiday Calendar (HR-HOL-2026). Floating holidays may be used on any working day for any reason, with manager approval. They do not carry over and are not paid out.

5.7 PARENTAL LEAVE
5.7.1 Eligibility
Employees become eligible for paid parental leave after 6 months of continuous service.
5.7.2 Entitlement
A birthing parent receives 12 weeks of paid parental leave. A non-birthing parent, including adoptive and foster parents, receives 6 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement.
5.7.3 Relationship to other leave
Paid parental leave runs at the same time as any job-protected leave the employee is entitled to under law. Employees should notify People Operations at least 30 days before the expected start date where possible.

5.8 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Employees receive up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, or sibling) and up to 3 days for other relatives. Bereavement leave does not reduce the PTO balance.

5.9 JURY DUTY
Employees summoned for jury duty receive their regular pay for up to 10 working days per leave year. Employees should share the summons with their manager as soon as they receive it.

Employees follow their base office calendar wherever they work. For example, a Pune-based employee working temporarily from Bengaluru does not get Kannada Rajyotsava as a holiday, and a Bengaluru-based employee visiting Austin works on US holidays unless they take leave.

6.8 CONTACT
For questions about this calendar, contact People Operations through AMS People.

7. REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK POLICY
Reference: HR-RWP-2026 | Version: 1.4 | Effective: 1 April 2026
7.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy explains when employees work from the office, when they may work from home or from another location, and what support AMS provides. Most roles at AMS are hybrid-eligible. Some roles are office-based because they need on-site equipment or presence, for example IT infrastructure, Facilities, and lab-based testing roles. The employee's offer letter or AMS People profile shows whether a role is hybrid-eligible.

7.2 HYBRID WORKING MODEL
7.2.1 Office days
Employees in hybrid-eligible roles work from their base office at least 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are company-wide anchor days when all hybrid employees are expected in the office. Each team chooses its third office day.
7.2.2 Working from home
On the remaining days, employees may work from home within their base city.
7.2.3 Core collaboration hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time in each office. Employees should be available for meetings during core hours on working days, whether in the office or at home.
7.2.4 Exceptions
A manager may approve a temporary reduction in office days, for example during recovery from an injury, for up to 8 weeks. Longer arrangements need People Operations approval.

7.3 WORKING FROM ANOTHER CITY (SAME COUNTRY)
7.3.1 Allowance
Employees may work from a city other than their base city, within the same country, for up to 30 working days per calendar year. Unused days do not carry over to the next year.
7.3.2 How to request
Employees submit a remote location request in AMS People at least 5 working days before the first remote day. The manager approves or declines the request.
7.3.3 Rules while working from another city
While working from another city, the employee keeps their base office's working hours, holiday calendar, and leave policy. Office anchor days do not apply during approved remote location days.

7.4 WORKING FROM ABROAD
7.4.1 Approval
Working from a country other than the country of the employee's payroll needs approval from the manager, People Operations, and Legal and Compliance. The request must be submitted in AMS People at least 30 calendar days before travel, because it can have tax and immigration consequences.
7.4.2 Limit
Working from abroad is limited to 15 working days per calendar year. These days are separate from the 30-day allowance for working from another city.
7.4.3 Not allowed
Working from abroad is not allowed from countries on the Legal and Compliance restricted list. Employees may not work while on approved leave abroad; leave days are recorded as leave, not as remote work.
7.4.4 Business travel is different
Travelling abroad for client work under an approved travel request (form TRV-F-02) is business travel under the Travel and Expense Policy, not working from abroad.

7.5 FULLY REMOTE ARRANGEMENTS
Fully remote work is an exception. It needs approval from the department head and People Operations and is reviewed every 6 months. A fully remote employee keeps a base office, follows that office's holiday calendar, and is expected to visit the office when asked, with travel paid under the Travel and Expense Policy.

7.6 EQUIPMENT AND ALLOWANCES
7.6.1 Equipment
AMS provides each employee with a laptop and the accessories needed for their role. Requests for additional equipment are raised through ServiceDesk.
7.6.2 Home office setup allowance
After completing probation, employees in hybrid-eligible roles receive a one-time home office setup allowance of INR 15,000 in India or USD 500 in the United States, claimed in AMS Expense with receipts.
7.6.3 Internet allowance
The monthly internet allowance for hybrid and remote employees is described in section 8.6 (Employee Benefits Policy, HR-BEN-2026).

7.7 INFORMATION SECURITY WHEN WORKING REMOTELY
7.7.1 Network
Employees must connect to the AMS VPN when working outside the office. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected.
7.7.2 Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone with AMS data must be reported to IT ServiceDesk within 24 hours at it-helpdesk@ams.example.
7.7.3 Confidential work
Employees should not take confidential client calls in public places and must lock their screen whenever they leave their device.

7.8 REMOTE WORK AND LEAVE
Working from home is not a form of leave. An employee who is too unwell to work should apply for sick leave rather than working from home. Holidays follow the base office calendar, so an employee working from their hometown does not get that city's local holidays.

7.9 CONTACT
For questions about this policy, contact People Operations through AMS People.

8. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY
Reference: HR-BEN-2026 | Version: 2.2 | Effective: 1 April 2026
8.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy describes insurance, learning, wellness, connectivity, and retirement benefits. Benefits differ between the India payroll and the US payroll; each section says which applies. Interns and contractors are not covered by this policy.

8.2 HEALTH AND INSURANCE BENEFITS (INDIA)
8.2.1 Group medical insurance
Employees on the India payroll are covered by a family floater medical policy with a sum insured of INR 5,00,000 per year. The policy covers the employee, spouse or partner, and up to two children. Coverage starts on the joining date.
8.2.2 Parents' cover
Employees may add parents or parents-in-law under an optional top-up policy. The premium for parents' cover is paid by the employee through salary deduction.
8.2.3 Enrolment and changes
Employees enrol dependants in AMS People within 30 days of joining. Dependants can be added later only after a life event, such as marriage or the birth or adoption of a child, and within 30 days of that event.
8.2.4 Claims
Cashless hospitalisation and reimbursement claims are handled by the insurer's third-party administrator, CareFirst TPA. The e-card is available in AMS People.
8.2.5 Life and accident cover
Employees are covered by group term life insurance of three times annual fixed pay and personal accident insurance of five times annual fixed pay. AMS pays the full premium.

8.3 HEALTH AND INSURANCE BENEFITS (UNITED STATES)
8.3.1 Medical plans
Employees on the US payroll choose between a PPO plan and a high-deductible health plan (HDHP) with a health savings account (HSA). AMS pays 90% of the employee premium and 70% of the premium for dependants.
8.3.2 HSA contribution
Employees enrolled in the HDHP receive an AMS contribution of USD 750 per year to their HSA, or USD 1,500 per year with family coverage.
8.3.3 Dental and vision
Dental and vision plans are available. AMS pays 100% of the employee premium for both.
8.3.4 Enrolment
New employees enrol within 30 days of their start date. Changes outside that window are allowed only during open enrolment in November or after a qualifying life event.

8.4 LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT BUDGET
8.4.1 Annual budget
Each employee has a personal learning budget. Employees on the India payroll receive INR 50,000 per financial year (1 April to 31 March). Employees on the US payroll receive USD 1,500 per calendar year.
8.4.2 What the budget covers
The learning budget covers online courses, certification exams, conference tickets, and work-related books. Books are limited to INR 5,000 or USD 100 per year within the budget. Travel to conferences is paid under the Travel and Expense Policy, not from the learning budget.
8.4.3 Carry-forward of unused budget
Up to 50% of the unused learning budget carries forward to the next budget year. Carried-forward budget must be used by the end of that next budget year; it cannot be carried forward a second time.
8.4.4 Approval and reimbursement
Learning expenses need manager approval in AMS People before payment. Employees are reimbursed after completing the course or exam and uploading the certificate or receipt.
8.4.5 Service agreement for costly certifications
A single certification or course costing more than INR 1,00,000 or USD 2,000 needs a service agreement. If the employee resigns within 12 months of completion, they repay a prorated share of the cost.

8.5 WELLNESS
8.5.1 Wellness allowance
Employees on the India payroll receive a wellness allowance of INR 12,000 per year, paid as INR 3,000 each quarter with salary. Employees on the US payroll receive a wellness allowance of USD 600 per year, reimbursed against receipts for gym memberships, fitness classes, or wellbeing apps. The wellness allowance does not carry over.
8.5.2 Employee Assistance Programme
All employees and their immediate family members can use the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP). The EAP provides up to 6 free, confidential counselling sessions per person per year and a 24-hour helpline. AMS does not receive the names of employees who use the EAP.

8.6 CONNECTIVITY ALLOWANCE
Employees in a hybrid or remote arrangement under the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy receive an internet allowance of INR 1,500 per month in India or USD 50 per month in the United States, paid with salary.

8.7 RETIREMENT BENEFITS
8.7.1 India
Employees on the India payroll contribute to the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) at 12% of basic pay, matched by AMS. Gratuity is payable on leaving after completing at least five years of continuous service.
8.7.2 United States
Employees on the US payroll may join the AMS 401(k) plan. AMS matches 100% of the employee's contribution up to 4% of eligible pay. Employer matching contributions vest immediately.

8.8 EMPLOYEE REFERRAL BONUS
Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus of INR 50,000 in India or USD 2,000 in the United States. The bonus is paid after the new hire completes 90 days of employment. Employees at grade L6, and members of the hiring panel for the role, are not eligible.

8.9 CONTACT
For questions about benefits, contact benefits@ams.example or raise a request in AMS People.

9. COMPENSATION AND BONUS POLICY
Reference: HR-CMP-2026 | Version: 1.3 | Effective: 1 April 2026
9.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy explains how pay is structured at AMS, when salaries are reviewed, and how the annual bonus works. Pay dates and payslips are covered in the HR FAQ.

9.2 PAY STRUCTURE
9.2.1 India
Pay in India has a fixed part and a variable part. Fixed pay includes basic pay (40% of fixed pay), house rent allowance (HRA, 50% of basic pay), and a flexible allowance. Provident fund contributions are 12% of basic pay, as set out in the Employee Benefits Policy (HR-BEN-2026).
9.2.2 United States
Pay in the United States is an annual base salary, paid biweekly, plus the annual bonus.

9.3 ANNUAL SALARY REVIEW
9.3.1 Timing
Salaries are reviewed once a year after the April performance rating. Increases take effect on 1 July and are shown in the increment letter in AMS People by 15 June.
9.3.2 Basis
The increase depends on the performance rating, the employee's position in the pay range for their grade, and the market. Employees rated "does not meet expectations" do not receive an increase.
9.3.3 Promotions
Promotion increases are separate from the annual review and take effect on the promotion date (Performance Review and Promotion Policy, HR-PRF-2026).

9.4 ANNUAL BONUS
9.4.1 Target bonus by grade
The target bonus is a percentage of annual fixed pay (India) or base salary (United States):
L1 and L2: 5%
L3 and L4: 10%
L5: 15%
L6: 20%
9.4.2 Actual bonus
The actual bonus depends on company results (from 0% to 150% of target) and the employee's rating. "Partially meets expectations" receives at most 50% of target; "does not meet expectations" receives no bonus.
9.4.3 Payment
The bonus for the financial year is paid with the June salary.
9.4.4 Eligibility
Employees must be employed and not serving a notice period on the payment date. Employees who joined during the financial year receive a prorated bonus from their joining date.

9.5 CONFIDENTIALITY
Salaries are confidential between the employee, their managers, and People Operations. Employees in the United States may discuss their own pay with colleagues, as US law protects this.

9.6 CONTACT
Payroll: payroll@ams.example. People Operations, through AMS People.

10. PERFORMANCE REVIEW AND PROMOTION POLICY
Reference: HR-PRF-2026 | Version: 2.0 | Effective: 1 April 2026
10.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy explains how performance is reviewed and rated at AMS, and how promotions work. Interns and contractors are not covered.

10.2 THE REVIEW CYCLE
10.2.1 Annual review (April)
Every employee receives an annual performance rating in April, covering the financial year that ended on 31 March. The employee writes a self-review in AMS People, the manager rates, and ratings are calibrated across each department in May.
10.2.2 Mid-year check-in (October)
In October, each manager holds a check-in with each team member on progress against goals. There is no rating in October.
10.2.3 Goals
Each employee agrees 3 to 5 goals with their manager in AMS People by 30 April.
10.2.4 New joiners
Employees who joined on or after 1 January are not rated in the April cycle; their first rating is in the following April.

10.3 RATINGS
AMS uses a five-point scale:
Outstanding
Exceeds expectations
Meets expectations
Partially meets expectations
Does not meet expectations
Ratings affect the annual salary increase and bonus (Compensation and Bonus Policy, HR-CMP-2026) and eligibility for internal job moves, which need "meets expectations" or higher.

10.4 PROMOTIONS
10.4.1 When
Promotions are reviewed twice a year, in April and October, and take effect from the first day of the following month, as set out in the Company Overview.
10.4.2 Eligibility
To be considered, an employee needs at least 12 months in their current grade on the first day of the cycle, a last rating of "meets expectations" or higher, and no overdue mandatory training (Mandatory Training Policy, LD-MAN-2026).
10.4.3 Nomination
The manager nominates the employee with a written case. Promotions to L5 and L6 are also reviewed by a panel of department heads.
10.4.4 Promotion increase
A promotion comes with a salary increase of 10% to 15% of fixed pay, effective from the promotion date.

10.5 PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLANS
An employee rated "does not meet expectations", or whose performance falls well below expectations during the year, may be placed on a performance improvement plan (PIP) of 60 days with clear goals and fortnightly check-ins. An employee on a PIP is not eligible for promotion or internal moves.

10.6 APPEALS
An employee who disagrees with their rating may appeal within 15 days of receiving it, through AMS People. The appeal is reviewed by the manager's manager and People Operations, who reply within 15 working days. Their decision is final.

10.7 CONTACT
People Operations, through AMS People.

11. TAX DECLARATIONS GUIDE (INDIA AND UNITED STATES)
Reference: HR-GDE-TAX | Version: 1.1 | Last updated: 1 April 2026
11.1 WHY THIS MATTERS
AMS deducts income tax from salary every month in India (TDS) and every payday in the United States (withholding). The right amount depends on information that only the employee can give.

11.2 INDIA: CHOOSING A TAX REGIME
11.2.1 Choice
Each financial year, employees on the India payroll choose between the new tax regime and the old tax regime in AMS People. The new regime is the default.
11.2.2 Deadline
Make the choice by 30 April. If no choice is made, payroll uses the new regime for the year.
11.2.3 Changing
The choice for salary deductions cannot be changed during the year, but the final choice can still be made when filing the personal tax return.

11.3 INDIA: INVESTMENT DECLARATIONS
11.3.1 Who needs them
Employees on the old regime declare planned tax-saving investments and expenses, such as life insurance premiums, the public provident fund (PPF), home loan interest, and rent for HRA, in AMS People by 30 April. On the new regime, most of these deductions do not apply.
11.3.2 Rent and HRA
To claim HRA, upload rent receipts. If annual rent is above INR 1,00,000, the landlord's PAN is also needed.
11.3.3 Proofs
Upload proofs of the declared investments between 1 and 31 January (Form 12BB). Declarations without proof by 31 January are removed, and the extra tax is deducted from the February and March salaries.

11.4 INDIA: AFTER THE YEAR
Form 16, which summarises salary and tax deducted, is available in AMS People by 15 June each year.

11.5 UNITED STATES
11.5.1 Form W-4
Employees on the US payroll complete Form W-4 in AMS People when they join. It can be updated at any time, for example after marriage or the birth of a child; the change applies from the next payday.
11.5.2 Form W-2
Form W-2 for the year is available in AMS People by 31 January.

11.6 HELP
Payroll answers questions about how AMS applies tax at payroll@ams.example but cannot give personal tax advice.

12. GRIEVANCE POLICY
Reference: HR-GRV-2026 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 1 April 2026
12.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
A grievance is a concern about your own work situation, such as workload, working hours, a change to your role, how a policy was applied to you, or a working relationship. This policy explains how grievances are raised and resolved.

12.2 WHAT IS NOT A GRIEVANCE
12.2.1 Harassment and discrimination
These follow section 2.5 of the Legal and Compliance Handbook (Code of Conduct): the POSH Policy (LEG-POSH-2026) in India and the US Equal Employment Opportunity Policy (LEG-EEO-US-2026) in the United States.
12.2.2 Wrongdoing
Fraud, bribery, data misuse, and similar concerns go through the Whistleblower and Ethics Line Procedure (LEG-WBL-2026).
12.2.3 Performance ratings
Rating disagreements follow the appeal process in the Performance Review and Promotion Policy (HR-PRF-2026).

12.3 THE THREE STAGES
12.3.1 Stage 1: talk to your manager
Raise the concern with your manager, in person or in writing. The manager responds within 5 working days. If the grievance is about your manager, go straight to stage 2.
12.3.2 Stage 2: People Operations
If stage 1 does not resolve it, raise a formal grievance in AMS People. A People Operations partner meets you and responds in writing within 10 working days.
12.3.3 Stage 3: grievance committee
If you are not satisfied with stage 2, ask for a review by the grievance committee (a department head from another department and a senior People Operations manager) within 10 working days. The committee's decision, given within 15 working days, is final.

12.4 YOUR RIGHTS
You may bring a colleague to any grievance meeting. Grievances are kept confidential, and you will not be treated unfairly for raising one in good faith.

12.5 CONTACT
People Operations, through AMS People.

13. HR FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Reference: HR-FAQ-2026 | Version: 1.2 | Last updated: 1 April 2026
This FAQ answers practical, everyday questions. It does not replace the policies. Where this FAQ and a policy appear to disagree, the policy applies.

13.1 LEAVE AND TIME OFF
13.1.1 How do I apply for leave?
Open AMS People, go to Leave, choose Apply, and select the leave type and dates. Your leave is approved only when the status changes to Approved. If AMS People is down, get written confirmation from your manager and enter the request once the portal is back.
13.1.2 What do I use if I need a day off at short notice?
In India, use casual leave, which is meant for short, unplanned personal matters. In the United States, use PTO, and let your manager know as early as you can. The exact entitlements are in your country's leave policy.
13.1.3 Is there bereavement leave in India?
The India leave policy does not have a separate bereavement leave type. You can use casual or annual leave. On request, People Operations may also approve up to 3 days of special paid leave after the death of an immediate family member.
13.1.4 I was absent but forgot to apply for leave. What now?
Apply for the missed day in AMS People within 7 calendar days. After 7 days, the missed day is treated as unpaid leave unless your manager and People Operations approve a late request.
13.1.5 What happens to my leave if I transfer from India to Austin?
On the transfer date, your India leave balance is closed and unused annual leave is settled under the India rules. You start on the US leave policy with a prorated PTO balance from the transfer date.

13.2 PAY AND PAYSLIPS
13.2.1 When is salary paid?
In India, salary is paid on the last working day of each month. In the United States, pay is every other Friday (biweekly).
13.2.2 Where can I find my payslips?
Payslips are in AMS People under Pay. In India, the payslip is published by the second working day of the following month. In the United States, it is published on payday.
13.2.3 How do I change my bank account?
Update your bank details in AMS People. Changes made by the 20th of the month apply to that month's salary. Changes made after the 20th apply from the following month.
13.2.4 When do I get my annual tax documents?
In India, Form 16 is available in AMS People by 15 June each year. In the United States, Form W-2 is available by 31 January each year.

13.3 OFFICE AND FACILITIES
13.3.1 I lost my access badge. What should I do?
Report it to Facilities at facilities@ams.example on the same day so the badge can be deactivated. Reception can give you a temporary day badge. A replacement badge costs INR 500 in India or USD 15 in the United States, deducted from salary.
13.3.2 Is there parking at the office?
Bengaluru: two-wheeler parking is free; car parking spaces are limited and allocated by Facilities, with priority for car pools. Pune: parking is free on a first-come, first-served basis. Austin: free parking is available in the building garage.
13.3.3 Is food provided at the office?
Bengaluru and Pune have a subsidised cafeteria where lunch costs INR 50 per meal. In Austin, a catered lunch is provided on the Tuesday and Thursday anchor days.
13.3.4 Can I bring a visitor to the office?
Register your visitor in AMS People by the end of the previous working day. Visitors must be escorted at all times and may not enter client project areas.

13.4 IT AND ACCESS
13.4.1 My laptop is broken. What do I do?
Raise a ServiceDesk ticket. IT aims to repair it or give you a replacement laptop within 2 working days.
13.4.2 Can I use my personal laptop or phone for work?
You may not store Confidential or Restricted information on personal devices. You may read work email and chat on your personal phone only through the AMS Mobile app, which IT manages.
13.4.3 I forgot my password.
Use the self-service reset link on the AMS sign-in page. If you are locked out after 3 failed attempts, raise a ServiceDesk ticket or call the IT help line.

13.5 CAREER AND LEARNING
13.5.1 How do I find out my grade?
Your grade is shown on your AMS People profile. The Company Overview describes what each grade means.
13.5.2 Can I use my learning budget for an MBA or other degree?
No. Degree programmes are not covered by the learning budget. AMS has a separate higher education sponsorship scheme for employees at grade L3 and above with at least 2 years of service. It covers up to 50% of tuition fees, needs a 24-month service agreement, and applications open each April.
13.5.3 Can I apply for a job in another team?
Yes. Internal openings are posted in AMS People. You can apply after 12 months in your current role if your last performance rating was "meets expectations" or higher. Your manager is told once you are shortlisted.

13.6 GETTING HELP
13.6.1 Who do I contact with an HR question?
Raise a request in AMS People. People Operations replies within 2 working days. For urgent matters, email People Operations directly.
13.6.2 How do I report a concern without giving my name?
Use the Ethics line described in section 2.6 of the Legal and Compliance Handbook (Code of Conduct). Reports can be anonymous.
13.6.3 Where can I get confidential counselling?
Use the Employee Assistance Programme described in section 8.5.2 (Employee Benefits Policy). AMS is not told who uses it.
