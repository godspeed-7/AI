EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (INDIA)
Policy ID: HR-LEAVE-IN-2024 | Version: 2.0 | Effective: 1 April 2024
Policy owner: People Operations | Applies to employees on AMS's India payroll (Bengaluru and Pune offices)

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy describes annual leave, sick leave, casual leave, parental leave, carry-over, encashment, and leave requests for permanent full-time employees on the India payroll. Interns and contractors follow their own agreements.

2. LEAVE YEAR AND GENERAL RULES
The leave year runs from 1 January through 31 December. Leave balances are recorded in days. Weekends and company public holidays within an approved leave period are not deducted from annual leave.
Employees submit leave requests in the HR portal. A request is approved only when the portal shows the status as approved.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE
3.1 Standard entitlement
Eligible permanent full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave for each complete leave year.
3.2 Joining during the year
Employees who join after 1 January receive a prorated annual-leave balance, calculated in the HR system from the joining date.
3.3 Annual leave approval
Employees should submit planned annual leave at least 7 calendar days before the first day of leave. Managers should respond within 5 working days.

4. ANNUAL LEAVE CARRY-OVER AND ENCASHMENT
4.1 Standard carry-over limit
Employees may carry over up to 8 unused annual-leave days from one leave year into the next leave year.
4.2 Conditions
The carried-over balance must be used by 30 June of the following year. Any carried-over annual leave remaining after 30 June expires. Sick leave and casual leave cannot be carried over.
4.3 No exceptions
Carry-over above the 8-day limit is not permitted under this version of the policy, including for business reasons.
4.4 Year-end encashment
At the end of each leave year, up to 10 unused annual-leave days that are not carried over are encashed automatically at basic pay and paid with the January salary.

5. SICK LEAVE
5.1 Standard entitlement
Eligible permanent full-time employees receive 10 days of sick leave per leave year.
5.2 Carry-over
Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next leave year.
5.3 Documentation
A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive working days.

6. CASUAL LEAVE
6.1 Standard entitlement
Eligible permanent full-time employees receive 7 days of casual leave per leave year.
6.2 Carry-over and conversion
Unused casual leave expires at the end of the leave year and cannot be carried over or encashed.

7. PARENTAL LEAVE
7.1 General approach
Parental-leave entitlements depend on the employee's circumstances and applicable local requirements. Employees should contact People Operations before planning parental leave.
