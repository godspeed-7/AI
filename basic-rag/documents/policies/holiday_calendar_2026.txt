HOLIDAY CALENDAR 2026
Document ID: HR-HOL-2026 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026
Document owner: People Operations | Applies to all AMS offices

TRAINING NOTE: This is a fictional document for RAG-learning exercises only. Festival dates are illustrative.

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This calendar lists the company holidays for 2026 for each AMS office. Employees follow the calendar of their base location, as defined in the Company Overview, even when they are working temporarily from another city or country. Company holidays are paid and are not deducted from any leave balance.

2. GENERAL RULES
2.1 Number of holidays
Each India office has 10 fixed holidays. Employees in India also choose 2 restricted holidays from the list in section 5. The Austin office has 10 fixed holidays, and US employees also receive 2 floating holidays under the US leave policy.
2.2 Holidays falling on a weekend
India offices: when a fixed holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, no substitute day off is given.
Austin office: when a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.
2.3 Working on a holiday
Employees asked by their manager to work on a company holiday receive a compensatory day off. In India, the compensatory day off must be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked and is recorded in AMS People as compensatory off. In Austin, exempt employees take the compensatory day within 90 days; non-exempt employees are paid for hours worked as required by law.
2.4 Holidays during leave
A company holiday that falls inside an approved leave period is not deducted from the leave balance.

5. RESTRICTED HOLIDAYS (INDIA OFFICES)
5.1 How restricted holidays work
In addition to the fixed holidays, each employee on the India payroll may choose 2 restricted holidays per calendar year from the list below. Employees apply for a restricted holiday in AMS People at least 7 calendar days in advance. Unused restricted holidays lapse at the end of the year and are not carried over or encashed.

7. EMPLOYEES WORKING AWAY FROM THEIR BASE OFFICE
Employees follow their base office calendar wherever they work. For example, a Pune-based employee working temporarily from Bengaluru does not get Kannada Rajyotsava as a holiday, and a Bengaluru-based employee visiting Austin works on US holidays unless they take leave.
